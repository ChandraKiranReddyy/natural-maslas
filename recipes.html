Pav Bhaji

Mashed mixed-vegetable curry, finished with a hot skillet of butter and served with toasted pav.

Pav Bhaji Masala Turmeric Powder Kashmiri Chilli
  1. Boil potatoes, cauliflower, peas and carrots until soft; mash roughly.
  2. Sauté onion, ginger-garlic and tomato in butter until the oil separates.
  3. Add 2 tbsp Pav Bhaji Masala, 1 tsp Turmeric Powder and 1 tsp Kashmiri Chilli; cook 2 minutes.
  4. Fold in the mashed vegetables, splash of water, simmer 10 minutes, mashing as it cooks.
  5. Toast buttered pav on a hot tawa; serve with raw onion and lemon.

Everyday Curry · North Indian

Home-Style Chicken Curry

A weeknight onion-tomato chicken curry, built on a proper masala base instead of a shortcut paste.

Chicken Masala Turmeric Powder Garam Masala
  1. Brown sliced onions in oil until deep golden; blend with ginger-garlic and tomato.
  2. Return the paste to the pan, add ½ tsp Turmeric Powder and 2 tbsp Chicken Masala; fry until the oil separates.
  3. Add chicken pieces, sear 5 minutes on high, then cover and cook 15 minutes.
  4. Add water to loosen, simmer to the gravy consistency you like.
  5. Finish with a pinch of Garam Masala and chopped coriander off the heat.

Punjabi Chole

Dark, tangy chickpea curry simmered with tea leaves for colour and a proper chole masala for depth.

Chana/Chole Masala Coriander Powder Dry Mango Powder
  1. Soak chickpeas overnight; pressure-cook with a tea bag until soft and dark.
  2. Sauté onion-tomato masala base until glossy.
  3. Stir in 2 tbsp Chana/Chole Masala and 1 tsp Coriander Powder; cook through.
  4. Add the cooked chickpeas with their liquid; simmer 15 minutes, mashing a few for body.
  5. Finish with ½ tsp Dry Mango Powder for tang; top with sliced onion and ginger.

Everyday Meal · South Indian

Vegetable Sambar

Lentil-and-vegetable stew, tempered twice, that turns rice into a full meal.

Sambar Masala Mustard Seeds Asafoetida
  1. Pressure-cook toor dal with turmeric until fully soft; whisk smooth.
  2. Boil chopped vegetables (drumstick, brinjal, pumpkin) in tamarind water until tender.
  3. Combine dal and vegetables; stir in 2–3 tbsp Sambar Masala and simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Temper Mustard Seeds and a pinch of Asafoetida in hot ghee; pour over the sambar.
  5. Rest 5 minutes before serving — sambar always tastes better a little settled.

Layered Chicken Biryani

Dum-cooked, two-layer biryani built on whole spices and one proper biryani masala — no shortcuts.

Biryani/Pulao Masala Whole Garam Spices Turmeric Powder
  1. Marinate chicken in yoghurt, 3 tbsp Biryani/Pulao Masala and ½ tsp Turmeric Powder for at least 2 hours.
  2. Par-boil basmati rice with whole cinnamon, cloves and bay leaf until 70% done.
  3. Layer marinated chicken, then rice, then fried onions and mint, in a heavy pot.
  4. Seal the lid with dough or foil; cook on high 5 minutes, then lowest heat 25 minutes.
  5. Rest off the heat 10 minutes before opening — this is what keeps the grains separate.
10 min
Everyday Ritual · All-India

Masala Chai

The two-minute recipe that outsells everything else in the house, done properly.

Chai Masala Green Cardamom
  1. Bring water, milk and 1 crushed Green Cardamom pod to a boil.
  2. Add tea leaves and ½ tsp Chai Masala; simmer 3 minutes.
  3. Add sugar to taste, simmer 1 more minute until the colour deepens.
  4. Strain into cups while it's still rolling — chai left standing goes flat.
